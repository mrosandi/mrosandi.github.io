02 SECTION

Overview

Connective Network is a social networking and community platform designed to eliminate digital fragmentation by consolidating communication, content, events, memberships, and commerce into a single ecosystem.

Organizations managing communities across multiple platforms face constant friction — notifications split across apps, data siloed in different systems, members experiencing inconsistent engagement

18SECTION

Key Learnings

Simplicity beats feature overload

We initially planned 10+ sub-dashboards. Testing proved that a unified channel feed is far more effective for user onboarding.

Community ownership drives trust

Allowing custom domains and Stripe pipeline direct routing made organization owners much more willing to invite high-value leads.

Gamification increases retention

Subtle user badges for event attendance boosted community participation metrics by over 20% during trial intervals.
